How it works

  1. Upload your agreement once.
    PDF or Word. Signature, name, date and placeholder fields like [Client Name] are detected for you.
  2. Open a file.
    Enter the client's name and email — or let your case management system do it through the API.
  3. The client signs.
    They get a link, review the agreement, adopt a signature and sign in about two minutes. Reminders go out automatically.
  4. You're done.
    The signed PDF and a certificate of completion with a full audit trail are stored on the file and sent to both sides.

Works with agreements it has never seen
